The upcoming laws in Manitoba for escooters

"Coming in 2027, Manitoba is introducing new rules under The Highway Traffic Act for Personal Electric Vehicles (PEVs) to help keep Manitobans safe when sharing roads, paths, and other public spaces.

What is a PEV? A Personal Electric Vehicle (PEV) is a small electric-powered vehicle used for personal transportation. Examples include: Electric kick-scooters (e-scooters) Electric skateboards Electric unicycles Hoverboards and self-balancing scooters Similar electric mobility devices Know Before You Ride Minimum Age PEV riders must be at least 16 years old. Helmet Required Everyone riding a PEV must wear a properly fitted helmet, including all passengers. Where You Can Ride PEVs can only be operated on roads with posted speed limits of 50 km/h or less, bicycle lanes, pathways, and cycling facilities (subject to local rules). PEVs are not allowed on sidewalks, unless signs specifically permit PEV use. Speed Limits PEVs have a maximum operating speed of 25 km/h. Ride Safe. Follow the Rules. PEV riders must follow the same basic rules of the road as other road users, including: Follow traffic signs and signals Ride in single file Use hand signals when turning or stopping Carry only the number of passengers the vehicle is designed for Do not use hand-held electronic devices while riding Do not use headphones or earphones in both ears while riding Do not ride while impaired by alcohol or drugs Use required lights and reflectors when riding at night Ride as close to the right curb as practicable Enforcement Police and other peace officers can stop PEV riders, enforce the rules, and inspect PEVs and safety equipment to ensure they meet legal requirements.
